# Asset provenance

- `assets/tarkit-logo.svg`: user-supplied `black logo En.svg`. Its original Hubot Sans text is preserved and the matching font is embedded so the supplied wordmark renders reliably as an image.
- `assets/favicon.svg`: symbol paths from the same user-supplied logo.
- Hubot Sans fonts: https://github.com/github/hubot-sans (SIL Open Font License in `assets/OFL.txt`). Regular, Bold and ExtraBold webfont files are local; the regular TTF is used for the sharing preview.
- Lucide 0.468.0: https://unpkg.com/lucide@0.468.0/dist/umd/lucide.min.js (ISC; license notice retained in the file).
- HubSpot, Salesforce, Notion, LinkedIn marks: https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/tree/11.15.0/icons — respective trademarks belong to their owners. Simple Icons is CC0.
- Apollo logo: https://www.apollo.io/_next/static/media/logo.0pq8s8~3b84w1.svg, discovered on Apollo's official website.
- Clay logo: https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/61477f2c24a826836f969afe/6778506d788ebf16fef48551_Clay%20primary%20logo.png, discovered on Clay's official website.
