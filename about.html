How we work

Separate evidence from assumptions

A buyer statement, a recorded result, and an untested idea are different inputs. Keep the distinction visible when choosing markets and messages.

Give the work an owner

Connect every workflow to the person who uses it, the information they need, and the point when a decision is reviewed.

Build for handover

Make the output understandable to the next person doing the work. The documented process should remain useful after the engagement ends.

Keep people responsible

Automation supports a defined task. People remain responsible for access decisions, acceptance criteria, and actions that affect customers.
