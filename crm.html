What makes TARKIT a B2B CRM?

TARKIT connects companies, contacts, stakeholder records, and opportunities. Your team can work across multiple relationships within an account, track deal stages and next actions, and continue into customer-success tracking after the win.

How do we get a proposal?

Talk to Sales about your team, workflows, implementation needs, and security requirements. We agree scope and commercial terms with you before any purchase.

What does iHelper do today?

iHelper provides role-aware, rule-based briefings from saved CRM data. It can summarize pipeline, targets, customers, next actions, and data quality. It does not make decisions or send messages for you. Generative AI account briefs are not yet connected in this version.

Are the forecasts AI predictions?

No. The six-month forecasts use saved opportunity values, expected close dates, and probabilities. Base, Aggressive, and Conservative scenarios help your team explore different assumptions; they are planning models, not guaranteed outcomes.

Can we explore TARKIT before talking to Sales?

Yes. Open the demo workspace to explore fictional accounts, opportunities, and activities. Demo changes stay in your browser tab session; they do not change your real CRM records.
