Privacy, in plain language.

How this website handles your visit and your enquiry.

Website visits

This release does not include analytics, advertising trackers, or website cookies. Fonts, icons, and logos are served with the site. The hosting provider may process request information, such as IP addresses, as part of operating the service.

Contact enquiries

The enquiry form prepares an email in your email application. The form does not submit information to a website database. If you send the message, Tarkit receives the contact information and enquiry you include and uses it to respond and discuss the requested work.

The CRM demo

The CRM demonstration is a separate site with fictional sample data. Its on-screen notice explains session storage. Do not enter confidential customer information into a public demonstration.

Questions and requests

Use our contact page to ask about information you shared with Tarkit or request correction or deletion.
