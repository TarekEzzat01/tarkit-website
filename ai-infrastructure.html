What needs to be ready before you automate?

Tarkit · Updated 1 October 2026

AI infrastructure is the data, integrations, permissions, evaluation, and operating process that lets an AI-enabled workflow work reliably. Start with a bounded task and a way to judge the output.

In this guideChoose a bounded use caseCheck data readinessDefine the system boundaryEvaluate before expandingPlan for operation

Choose a bounded use case

Name the input, expected output, user, and decision. Preparing an account briefing is more actionable than “add AI to sales.” Decide which steps require human review.

Check data readiness

Locate the source records, confirm ownership and access rights, and assess quality. Missing or outdated records do not become reliable when placed in a model prompt.

Define the system boundary

Map the services that receive data, the credentials they need, and what they are allowed to do. Keep secrets out of public website code and give integrations only the access needed.

Evaluate before expanding

Build a representative set of examples. Agree what acceptable output looks like, how failures are detected, and how a person can review or recover the workflow.

Plan for operation

Record owners, logging, cost controls, fallback behavior, and a review cadence. Recheck the workflow when source systems, models, or business rules change.

Bring it into your next review

Write down the decision you need to make, the evidence you have, and who owns the next action. That is a useful place to start.
